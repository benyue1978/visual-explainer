**Short description**

Compare decimal and binary data-size prefixes, from kB and KiB to MB and MiB.

**Alt text**

A warm ivory infographic shows two diverging staircases from one byte. The decimal path multiplies by 1,000 per step, reaching 1 MB = 1,000,000 bytes. The binary path multiplies by 1,024 per step, reaching 1 MiB = 1,048,576 bytes. A callout gives their 48,576-byte difference.
