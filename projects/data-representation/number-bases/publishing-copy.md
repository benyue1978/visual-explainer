**Alt text**

A warm ivory infographic follows 173 in denary into the binary pattern 10101101. Place values 128, 32, 8, 4, and 1 are highlighted and sum to 173. The same bits are grouped as 1010 and 1101, which map to hexadecimal A and D, giving AD.
