## Website

**Short description**

Two step-by-step methods convert the same whole number between binary and denary.

**Alt text**

A warm ivory infographic converts 110101 in binary to 53 in denary by aligning the bits with powers of 2 and adding 32, 16, 4, and 1. It then converts 53 back to 110101 by repeatedly dividing by 2 and reading the remainders from bottom to top. A note shows that zero is written as 0 in both systems.
